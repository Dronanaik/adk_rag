    An enterprise-ready Hybrid Document Intelligence Platform built with Google Agent Development Kit (ADK). Authenticate with Neon PostgreSQL, ingest 13 document formats through a live 5-step dual vector + Neo4j graph pipeline, persist multi-session chat & long-term memories, explore interactive knowledge topologies, and converse in real time with a Gemini Live Voice Agent.
